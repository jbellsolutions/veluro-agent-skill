## Read the evidence labels

**Tested** means executed and checked in this account. **UI observed** means a screen or control was inspected, but its final effect may not have been exercised. **Source observed** means the shipped browser code contains the behavior; it is not a backend guarantee. **Vendor described** means the help or demo describes it. **Unverified / gated** means production behavior could not be established here.

The documentation baseline is comprehensive across the discovered client modules. Production certification is still open for live advertising, outbound delivery, account connections, generated media, public publishing, and unattended optimization. These flows require real connected services and appropriate campaign scope. No documentation claim treats an unexecuted flow as tested.

## Important findings

1. Direct referral commission is 20%. The account's reseller terms show **10% of a recruited reseller's subscription plus 5% of their client revenue**. The demo confirms that distinction.
2. Partner Growth has finite allowances. “Unlimited service” needs to mean an operating offer within the client's purchased limits and approved external costs, rather than unlimited platform consumption.
3. Meta's integrations screen says general one-click ad launching and auto-posting await app review. Local Listings is Coming Soon. LinkedIn Compose is manual-send copy.
4. Business Health returned substantial revenue and campaign figures despite this account having no connected ad or financial services and zero campaigns. Treat those values as untrusted until their origins are reconciled.
5. The app's internal authenticated API works. A draft was created in the UI, read and updated through the API, confirmed in the UI, and deleted. No ad was launched; no AI credit was consumed. A published, versioned developer API contract was not located.
